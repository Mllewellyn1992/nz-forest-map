Sources and limits

  • Forests: Manaaki Whenua Land Cover Database v6 (2023/24 map). Plantation = exotic forest + harvested forest; mānuka/kānuka = class 52. Touching areas are joined into blocks; only blocks of 50 ha+ are shown. Plantation blocks total ha, against MPI's 1,818,434 ha net stocked (1 April 2025).
  • Planting period comes from when each piece of land first shows as forest across the 1996-2023 land cover maps. It is an upper bound: land harvested and replanted between two maps looks older than it is. "Harvest age" = planted before 2001 (25+ years).
  • Titles, forestry rights and Crown forest licences: LINZ open data (parcels, titles, title instruments). Owner names are not shown: LINZ only releases them under a separate licence.
  • Who manages: every current FSC forest-management certificate in NZ (search.fsc.org) and each firm's own published forest list. A tick means the hectares were re-read at the source by hand. These are not placed on the map: firms name forests, not boundaries.
  • Overseas buys: Overseas Investment Office forestry consents 2005-2026 that could be placed from the address in the decision (208 of 580). A dot marks the address, not the whole forest bought.
  • This is a market view of who is around. It is not a contact list.
