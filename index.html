NZ forests, 10 ha+

How up to date is each part?

ForestsLand cover mapped in 2023/24. Blocks of 10 ha+ only. Plantation total ha (MPI: 1.82m ha stocked; the land-cover map also counts felled and unstocked land).
Harvest dueWorked out for each 30 m patch. Where the satellite saw it felled (2001-2025, Global Forest Watch) the age is exact. Elsewhere it comes from tree height on LINZ lidar, or the 1996-2023 land-cover maps, scaled so each region's age mix matches MPI's (NEFD); the tallest patches get the oldest ages. Due = planted + 28 years. Zoom in to see each patch. An estimate, not a harvest plan. Felled 2024-25 = cut in the last two years.
Carbon landPost-1989 forest land from MfE's LUCAS 2020 land use map: forest on land that was not forest at the end of 1989, the land that can earn carbon units in the ETS. Planted on farmland 2018-23 = grass or scrub on every land-cover map to 2018, forest in 2023.
Manager on mapA firm's named forests placed by LINZ place names. Covers about a third of plantation area, so "Not matched" does not mean no manager.
ManagersEach firm's latest public list. The date is on every firm; older = before 2025, owner changed = sold since the list.
Top heightLINZ lidar, flown 2013-2026 (year on each forest). The height in the year it was flown, not today. Top height is the foresters' measure of the tallest trees: here the average of the tallest point in each 10 x 10 m square, read from the full 1 m lidar, for every 80 m patch (in patchy stands, the top 1% of the canopy less 1.3 m). The card gives the block's middle patch and its tallest tenth, and the average canopy (gaps and edges included, about 3-4 m lower). At age 20 it matches NZ growth plots within about 1.5 m in Hawke's Bay, Gisborne and Northland. Not checked against ground plots. Forests with under 5% flown show none.
Height todayAn estimate, not measured: each 80 m patch's top height grown on from its lidar year to today with the published NZ radiata growth curve (van der Colff & Kimberley 2013), using each region's typical site quality learned from patches of known age. Patches clear-felled since the flight (satellite) count as regrowing from replanting. Real stands vary; thinning and storm damage are not seen.
Wood at harvestMPI yield tables (NEFD 2015) for the region, radiata planted after 1989, age 28, pruned and unpruned. A regional average, not measured on the forest. No table for the West Coast.
PortsMPI forestry trade statistics, the last four quarters (Jul 2025 - Jun 2026), by port and product.
MillsMills with a dated 2025-26 sign they are running (news, company post or job ad), or a Google Maps listing marked open with a review in the last 12 months, checked 24 Nov 2026. Small mills with no recent source are left out, so the list is not complete.
Drive timeCar drive areas of 15-60 min around each port and mill (OpenStreetMap roads, Valhalla). A loaded log truck is slower, so treat these as best case. A forest counts when the drive area reaches any part of it (its nearest edge).
New plantingPilot, Central Hawke's Bay hill farmland only (about 1,000 km²; tick "Areas checked" to see it). Each 2 km square of the 2025-26 LINZ aerial photos (0.2 m, shown at 1.33 m) was read by an AI model (Claude) for planted forest outside the mapped forests, then marked in 1 ha squares: young planting (rows of small trees or fresh planting spots) and older pine stands the land-cover map missed (each older stand checked on close-up photos; native bush taken out). Edges are blocky (100 m) and some stands are missed.
FSCCurrent certificates, read 23 Nov 2026.
OwnersLand owner names are not shown (LINZ licence needed).
